# Race preparation

What is planned between today and a target race, on top of "Races" in `coach-rules` and [season.md](season.md): which plan carries the work at this distance in time, what the last weeks and days hold, how rest and form shape a taper, and what to do when illness or a late entry gets in the way. An athlete rule replaces any figure here.

The size of a taper is the taper default in `coach-rules`. Everything in this file is convention: this suite's own choice. Say "convention" when form moves a taper.

## How far out

Count whole weeks from the coming Monday, or from today when today is a Monday, to the Monday of race week; in race week the count is 0. An A race's taper weeks are the ones "Races" gives it, race week included.

| The race is | The work is carried by | What to settle now |
|---|---|---|
| Beyond the current block and the next | the season plan | the race's week roles and phases; nothing below that |
| Inside the current block or the next, before its taper or race week | the month plan | the load weeks left, their key sessions, the last long sessions and the taper weeks |
| An A race inside its taper; a B race in its race week or the week before; a C race in its week | the week plans and day plans | the days |

## Choosing priorities

Recommend a priority for every race, with one line of reason each. The athlete's "ok" selects them, and setup records them. Ask once, in one message, only when the goals in the profile do not tell which race the season is for.

- **A:** the race the season is built for. Two in twelve months as a rule. Eight load weeks or more between the recovery of one and the taper of the next is comfortable.
- **B:** a race worth a short rest. It fits best 3 to 6 weeks before an A race of the same sport or of one of its legs, at the same distance or shorter, and not in the week before that race's taper starts.
- **C:** a race trained through: one the athlete enters for practice.
- **A late entry:** a race entered with fewer than four load weeks before its taper would start is recommended as B, or as C when "Ready for the distance" is not met. Say why.
- Races too close to each other, and a race inside another's taper or recovery: as "Clashes" in [season.md](season.md) says.

A priority the athlete states stands: prepare the race at it, and say the recommendation in one line. A race with none yet is prepared at the priority recommended here; say so. Approval of the preparation is approval of that priority.

## Ready for the distance

For a run of 15 km or more, and for a half or full triathlon, check whether the long sessions can get there.

1. What the race asks: the long-run and long-ride shares in "What training since then shows" in [prediction.md](prediction.md), with the bike time from the goal or from `coach-predict`.
2. What the athlete can reach: start from the longest run and ride that the long-run limit and the long-ride default of `coach-rules` count from, and grow each by that limit and that default through the load weeks from this one to the last load week before the taper, or before race week for a B or C race. Easy weeks do not grow.
3. Where one cannot reach what the race asks, say so with both figures. Recommend the race as B or C, or as a training day, and leave the choice to the athlete. The limits are not stretched to fit a race.

A long run that stops at the long-run ceiling is as ready as the rules allow: say that the ceiling stands and what the race will ask beyond it.

## Key sessions in the build

One line each for the month plan's rows, by the next A or B race the block builds to; the week plan writes the sessions.

| Race | Each load week holds |
|---|---|
| 10 km | reps at threshold, and reps at 10 km pace in the last 6 weeks before race week |
| Half marathon | a tempo run, and a long run with its last third at race pace every second week |
| Marathon | a long run with blocks at marathon pace every second week, with the fuelling of race day practised in it |
| Sprint or Olympic triathlon | a brick, bike intervals at race power, and open water once it is available |
| Half or full triathlon | a long ride with blocks at race power and race fuelling, a brick off it every second week, and open water once it is available |

## The last long sessions

For an A race. A B or C race changes no long session beyond what "Races" gives its week.

- **The longest run and the longest ride** fall in the last load week at the latest, and no later than 21 days before a marathon or a full-distance triathlon.
- **The last hard session at full length** falls in the last load week. Inside the taper every hard session is a shortened one.

A session the plan holds later than this is proposed for a move earlier or a cut.

## The taper by week

For an A race, the cut in volume of each taper week, inside the taper default's range and against its baseline:

| Taper | Cut |
|---|---|
| One week | 50% |
| Two weeks | 41%, then 60% in race week |
| Three weeks | 41%, 50%, then 60% in race week |

Race week's cut covers Monday to the day before the race; the race is not counted. The sessions of a taper week add up to its cut: shorten each by about the same share, the long session by more. A taper week that was already trained at a load week's volume is left as it was: nothing more is cut from the weeks after it.

## Rest in the taper

- One full rest day or more in each taper week. The athlete's fixed rest day counts.
- The day after a shortened hard session is easy or rest.
- A readiness verdict of MODIFY or SKIP in race week is followed that day; nothing is made up.
- Say once, when the taper starts, that sleep is part of it: the usual hours or more, every night of race week.

## Race week by day

Counted back from race day, for an A race. A day that falls before race week's Monday keeps the plan of its own week. A day with nothing in its row is easy, 30 to 45 minutes in all. A B race keeps its week from "Races" and takes only the day-before row.

| Day | Holds |
|---|---|
| 6 and 5 days before | easy sessions |
| 4 or 3 days before | the week's short hard session: half the work of a normal one or less, at race pace or power |
| 2 days before | rest; 30 minutes very easy where race week already holds a full rest day |
| The day before | openers: 20 to 30 minutes easy with 4 to 6 pickups of 20 to 30 seconds. A triathlon: a short swim, a short ride with pickups that also checks the bike, and an optional 10-minute run |
| Race day | the race, as its planned session gives it |

- **An athlete rule on the week's shape** (a long-run day, a fixed rest day) stands. Lay the week out with it. Where it cannot hold beside this table, name the clash in one line with what the table would put there, and ask which the athlete wants; the rule stays until they say.
- The day before and race morning: food is `coach-nutrition`'s "Before a race". Kit laid out, the bike checked, transitions walked, nothing new.
- Targets for the race come from its planned session. Offer `coach-predict` where the athlete asks what to expect.

## Form in the taper

Read the load state when the taper starts and again on the Monday of race week.

| Form | Taper |
|---|---|
| Below -20 when the taper starts | 60% from the first taper week, and its first two days easy |
| -20 to 0 | as "The taper by week" gives it |
| Above 0 when the taper starts | the athlete is already fresh: 41% in every taper week but the last, 50% in race week, so that fitness is not given away |
| Below -10 on the Monday of race week | race week's short hard session is cut to 10 minutes of work or less; the openers stay |

Form names the depth of the cut. It adds no session and no load.

## Illness or a gap before a race

Inside the three weeks before an A or B race, these come first, in this order. What the athlete says about how they feel is logged first, as the subjective log in `coach-format` says.

1. The fever limit and its return, and the missed-session limit, as `coach-rules` gives them. A race does not shorten the return.
2. The gap default does not apply: the taper goes on from today's date. After 5 days or more without training, a taper week before race week takes the 41% cut on the days that are left; race week stays as "The taper by week" gives it.
3. After a fever, race week's short hard session happens only when an easy session two days before it felt normal; otherwise the openers alone.
4. Where the last day of the return is fewer than 5 days before race day, recommend racing it as a C race or not starting. Otherwise the race keeps its priority.
5. Offer `coach-predict` for a new figure and, where it has moved off the goal, a new goal through setup.

Whether to start is the athlete's decision, and what the illness was is for a clinician.

## The plan that already exists

Compare what exists with what the race asks, and list each difference:

- a week whose role in the season or month plan is not the one the race fixes;
- a taper or race week planned at a load week's volume;
- a long or hard session later than "The last long sessions" allows;
- a race-week day that differs from "Race week by day" or from "Rest in the taper".

Change no more than the race asks.
