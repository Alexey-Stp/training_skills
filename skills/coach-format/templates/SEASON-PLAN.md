- Load pattern: three load weeks, then an easy one (the default)
- Target races and goals: see the athlete profile

## Why this season looks like this

Counted back from the three target races. The twenty weeks to the 10 km divide into five blocks of three load weeks and a fourth week, and the race week takes the place of the last easy week. Only four weeks lie between the 10 km and the triathlon's taper, so they are a block of two load weeks and an easy one, then one load week before the taper. After the triathlon, nine weeks lead to the half marathon's taper: the last block keeps three load weeks, and the two before it have two each. The run focus in the winter and before the half marathon is the athlete's choice, kept from the earlier plan. All of this is convention.
