# Athlete rules

The athlete's own rules. They override the coaching rules where the two differ. All values in this example are invented.

## Limits (never planned past without my say-so)

- Knee pain 4 or more out of 10 on waking: no running that day.
- Knee pain 2 or 3: easy running only, flat, at most 45 min.

## Targets

- Bike targets derive from CP.
- Run easy days are capped by heart rate (under 150 bpm), not pace.

## Readiness bands

| Score | Readiness verdict |
|---|---|
| 70 and above | go |
| 50 to 69 | modify |
| below 50 | modify; skip only with a symptom or a second poor day |

## Weekly shape

- Saturday club ride is fixed and counts as the long ride.
- Friday is always a rest day.
- No hard session the day after the long run.

## Departures from the coaching rules

- Easy week at 70% of the previous week, not 60%.
- Non-focus sports during a run focus: swim twice a week, not once.

## Routines (reminders only)

- Hip and core routine, 10 min, daily.

## Kept from the earlier setup

- Session reviews of easy rides skip the interval breakdown.
